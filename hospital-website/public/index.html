About Us

A multi specialty hospital in the heart of Palakurthy

Eternal Multi Specialty Hospital is located opposite the Bus Stand on Jangaon Road, Palakurthy. The hospital offers Pediatrics care with Dr. M. Naresh (M.B.B.S, DCH, MIAP) and General Medicine with Dr. M. Haritha (M.B.B.S, MD General Medicine, Fellowship in Echocardiography).

The hospital provides 24/7 emergency services and has a pharmacy at its entrance.
